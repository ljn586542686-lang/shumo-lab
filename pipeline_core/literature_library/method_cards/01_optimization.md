# Optimization Methods

- Applicable problems: continuous optimization, resource allocation, scheduling.
- Core idea: define decision variables, objective functions, constraints, and solve feasible optima.
- Common algorithms: nonlinear programming, genetic algorithm, particle swarm, simulated annealing, multi-start local search.
- Strengths: clear objective and constraints.
- Limitations: local optima, sensitivity to bounds and initialization.
- Paper use: model construction, algorithm design, sensitivity analysis.
