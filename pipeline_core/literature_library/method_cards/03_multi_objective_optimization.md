# Multi-objective Optimization

- Applicable problems: competing objectives, balance and efficiency tradeoffs.
- Core idea: combine objectives through weights, Pareto search, or lexicographic rules.
- Common algorithms: weighted sum, epsilon constraint, NSGA-style genetic search.
- Strengths: exposes tradeoffs.
- Limitations: weight choices need sensitivity checks and human justification.
- Paper use: objective design and robustness discussion.
